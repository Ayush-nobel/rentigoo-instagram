# Daily 20-minute routine

You do all of this yourself, by hand, in the Instagram app. Nothing here is automated (that breaks Instagram's rules and gets accounts blocked).

Best time: 30 minutes before or after the day's post (7-9 pm), when people are on the app.

## 1. Reply to everyone (5 min)
- [ ] Reply to **every comment** on the last 3 posts. A question back ("Kis function ke liye chahiye?") keeps the thread going, and Instagram counts it.
- [ ] Reply to **every DM** and message request. Rental questions: answer, then send them the WhatsApp number (+91 93014 22919) or the listing link.
- [ ] Anyone asking for something not listed: note it in a list of requests. That's what to recruit owners for.

## 2. Ten genuine comments on Bhopal accounts (10 min)
- [ ] Open 10 posts from local accounts in `growth/accounts.csv` (event pages, cafés, photographers, colleges, Bhopal meme and news pages).
- [ ] Write a real comment on each: something about the post itself, 6+ words. No "Nice 🔥", no links, no "check our page".
  - Good: "Yeh Upper Lake wala shot kis time liya? Light kamaal hai."
  - Good: "Fest ki date kab announce hogi? Last year ka stage setup mast tha."
- [ ] Never copy-paste the same comment twice.
- [ ] New good local account found? Add it to `growth/accounts.csv`.

## 3. Stories (5 min)
- [ ] 1-3 stories a day. Ideas, all quick:
  - Re-share today's post with a question sticker ("Kya rent karoge is weekend?")
  - A poll: "Shaadi ke liye camera: kharido ya rent karo?"
  - Behind the scenes: you photographing a new listing, packing an item, a hand-over (with permission)
  - A new listing on rentigoo.in (screenshot + link sticker)
- [ ] Add anything useful to a Highlight: How it works, Deposits, New listings, FAQ.

## Once a week (Sunday, 15 min)
- [ ] Read the Monday report in `reports/` and pick one thing to do more of.
- [ ] Send 5 outreach messages from `growth/outreach.md`, by hand, and update `status` in `growth/accounts.csv`.
- [ ] Check next week's posts in `output/previews.html`. Film the 2 reels from their shot lists in `reels/`.

## Don'ts
- No follow/unfollow tricks, no buying followers or likes, no engagement pods, no comment or DM bots.
- Don't DM people who haven't interacted with you, apart from the businesses and pages in `outreach.md`, one message each, by hand.
